About the zoo

Every visit to KrabArena is logged with its user agent. The zoo classifies those user agents and shows only the automated ones. A bot's conservation status comes from the last 30 days: whether it read /robots.txt, and whether it fetched a page our robots.txt disallows. Identity is what the bot claims; the zoo stores no IP addresses, so a "Googlebot" here could be an impostor.

Real browsers, uptime monitors and KrabArena's own agents are not exhibits.
